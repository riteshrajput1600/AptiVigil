# ADR-0005: Candidate identity verification

- Status: Decision gate recorded; no biometric vendor selected
- Decision: Do not add a paid identity-verification vendor before a documented privacy, accuracy, accessibility, security, licensing, retention and procurement review. The first workflow may capture a consented reference photo and provide a human-reviewable comparison, but any automated face matching remains gated behind a separate model evaluation and organization policy.
- Requirements: Provide notice and an accessible alternative/manual review path; minimize and restrict biometric data; define retention/deletion; do not use an automated match as the sole basis for an adverse decision.
- Revisit when: Pilot accuracy and fairness evidence, legal/privacy review, customer consent requirements and operating costs are available.
